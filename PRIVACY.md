# PO Calculator – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

PO Calculator ("the app") is an offline Post Office savings calculator made by
Mahesh Rai. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.**
It has no internet permission, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your phone

- **Saved accounts** you choose to save (a name you type, scheme, amount,
  opening date and rate) and your settings (language, theme, any interest
  rates you type in) are stored only on your phone, inside the app.
- They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- The developer cannot see them.

## Things that happen only when you tap them

- **Backup file:** "Save backup file" creates a file of your saved accounts and
  opens your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes (for example your
  own WhatsApp, email or Google Drive). "Restore" reads a file you pick.
- **Share:** sharing a result, scheme details, a form link or the app link
  sends text or an image through the app you choose.
- **Official forms:** Download opens the form PDF in your phone's browser.
  The PDFs are the Department of Posts specimen forms (SB Order No.
  35/2020), kept in this app's public GitHub project
  (raw.githubusercontent.com), because India Post's own form links expire.
- **Official sources and web pages:** these open in your phone's browser on
  government websites (indiapost.gov.in, dea.gov.in and
  incometaxindia.gov.in). Those sites, and GitHub,
  have their own privacy policies.
- **Suggestions and bug reports:** these open your own e-mail app with a
  message to the developer. If you send it, the developer receives your
  e-mail address, your message, the app version and the app language. It is
  used only to reply to you and improve the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** – to remind you before a saved account matures (only if
  you turn reminders on).
- **Run at startup** – to keep those reminders after the phone restarts.
- The app does **not** request internet, location, contacts, camera,
  microphone or storage permissions.

## Children

The app is a general calculator for adults and post office staff. It does not
knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Not affiliated

PO Calculator is an independent app. It is not a government app and is not
affiliated with, or endorsed by, India Post, the Department of Posts, the
National Savings Institute or the Government of India. Results are estimates;
confirm at your post office.
