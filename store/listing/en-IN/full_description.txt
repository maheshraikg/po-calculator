PO Calculator is a free, offline Post Office savings calculator in 11 Indian languages: English, Kannada, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Malayalam, Odia and Punjabi. Work out the maturity amount, interest and maturity date for every Post Office small savings scheme in seconds, without internet.

SCHEMES
• Recurring Deposit (RD) calculator
• Time Deposit (TD / Post Office FD) 1, 2, 3 and 5 years
• Monthly Income Scheme (MIS)
• Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)
• National Savings Certificate (NSC)
• Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)
• Public Provident Fund (PPF)
• Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
• Savings Account (SB) and Mahila Samman (MSSC, existing accounts)

WHAT YOU GET
• Maturity value, total interest and maturity date
• Year-wise table and growth chart
• Interest for each financial year with an estimated tax at your slab, and the TDS rules
• Premature closure amount for any date, including the 5-year TD rule and the official KVP table
• PPF and SCSS extension, PPF and SSY withdrawal limits
• Compare schemes side by side for the same amount
• Interest rates since 2016, so older accounts use the rate on their opening date
• Save your accounts, get a reminder before maturity, and back them up to a file
• Share a clean result card on WhatsApp

FOR POST OFFICE STAFF AND AGENTS
• Eligibility check and document checklist
• Step-by-step account opening guide for every scheme
• Full scheme details you can send to a customer in their language
• Official forms (Account Opening, KYC, SB-7, SB-7B, SB-7C and more) to download or send
• Enter a new quarter's interest rates as soon as they are announced

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No login, no internet permission, works offline. Your saved accounts stay on your phone.
